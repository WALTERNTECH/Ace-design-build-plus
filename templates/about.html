Our Story

Founded on a Fundamental Belief.

ACE Design + Build Partners was founded in Nairobi in 2020 with a singular premise: that the separation of design and construction creates inefficiency, conflict, and waste.

Traditional procurement forces architects and contractors into an adversarial relationship — one draws it, the other builds it, and nobody is fully accountable for the result.

“We eliminated the gap. One team carries your project from first site visit to final key. No handovers. No blame. Just delivery.”

Today, our multidisciplinary team works in concert across every project type — from luxury residential compounds in Karen to industrial masterplans in the Horn of Africa.
