What We Do

Six disciplines.
One delivery team.

Take the whole scope or a single stage. Every discipline below is staffed in-house and licensed under a recognised professional body, so the work never passes through a firm we do not control.

Service 01

Architectural Design

Residential, commercial and mixed-use developments taken from concept through to authority-approved documentation. Every drawing set is signed and stamped by AAK-registered architects, and coordinated against structure and services before it leaves the office.

  • Concept development and site planning
  • BIM and Revit modelling
  • Planning approvals and statutory submissions
  • 3D visualisation and construction documentation
Service 02

Structural Engineering

Reinforced concrete and steel systems designed for safety, durability and buildability. Calculations are certified by IEK-affiliated engineers who sit in the same office as the architects — which is why our drawings rarely need a redesign once they reach site.

  • Foundation and substructure design
  • Reinforced concrete and steel structures
  • Seismic analysis and structural assessment
  • Certified engineering reports
Service 03

Construction Management

NCA Category 4 registration authorises ACE to self-perform large-scale building works. Each project runs under a PMP®-certified construction manager working to ACMK's code — with cost reporting a client can read without a quantity surveyor sitting next to them.

  • Site supervision and programme management
  • Procurement and subcontractor coordination
  • Transparent cost reporting and valuations
  • Quality assurance and site safety
Service 04

Interior Fit-Out

Corporate and residential interiors delivered to a written specification rather than a mood board. Our fit-out division handles everything after the shell — so the finish quality is held by the same firm that built the structure behind it.

  • Office and corporate fit-outs
  • Custom millwork and acoustic ceilings
  • Lighting design and furniture planning
  • Premium finishing works and handover
Service 05

Diaspora Real Estate

Building in Nairobi from abroad removes your ability to walk the site, so we replace it with documentation. Monthly reporting, verified drawdowns against completed work, and one named contact who answers in your timezone.

  • Monthly photographic and written progress reports
  • Drawdowns released against verified completed work
  • Land acquisition and due-diligence support
  • A single named point of contact throughout
Service 06

Industrial Masterplanning

Plant and processing facilities planned around throughput, logistics and secure value chains — functional zoning, production cores and phased expansion, with the civil and structural work to build them.

  • Functional zoning and site logistics
  • Production core and systems integration planning
  • Phased technical expansion and support facilities
  • Site preparation, foundations and commissioning support
How We Deliver

Five stages,
one programme.

The same sequence on every project, whether it is a private villa or a 42,000 sq ft commercial block. You always know which stage you are in and what has to close before the next one opens.

STAGE 01

Consultation

Project briefing, site analysis and feasibility. We establish what the site can carry and what the budget can reach before anyone draws anything.

STAGE 02

Design & Approvals

Concept through to detailed drawings, structural coordination and statutory approvals — submitted under AAK and IEK registration.

STAGE 03

Costing & Contract

Bills of quantities and a cost plan from our BORAQS-registered surveyors, so the contract price rests on measured work rather than an estimate.

STAGE 04

Construction

Execution under a PMP®-certified manager, with programme tracking, procurement, quality assurance and monthly cost reporting.

STAGE 05

Handover & Support

Snagging, commissioning, as-built documentation and a defects liability period — the project is not closed until the paperwork is.
