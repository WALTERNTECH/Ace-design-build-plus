Professional Affiliations

ACE operates under the full regulatory and professional framework of Kenya's built environment. Every registration is current, independently verifiable and renewed on schedule — protecting our clients and our workers equally.

01 Regulatory & Licensing Bodies

Regulatory Body · Mandatory State Registration
National Construction Authority (NCA)

The mandatory state regulatory body in Kenya. All local contractors must affiliate with and be licensed by the NCA to legally operate, bid on, or execute building and civil engineering projects. ACE holds an active NCA Category 4 registration — the classification that authorises us to self-perform large-scale building works across Kenya. Independently verifiable through the Authority's public contractor register.

Category 4 Registered Annual Renewal Publicly Verifiable Mandatory Compliance
02 Employer & Contractor Associations
KABCEC
Member
● Active
Employer Association · Building & Civil Engineering
Kenya Association of Building and Civil Engineering Contractors

An influential employers' organisation that negotiates Collective Bargaining Agreements (CBAs) with trade unions and advises on Conditions of Contract for Building Works across Kenya. KABCEC membership means ACE operates under a structured, legally compliant employment framework — protecting both our workers and our clients' legal exposure on every site.

CBA Compliant Conditions of Contract Worker Protection

● Active
Professional Association · Construction Project Management
Association of Construction Managers of Kenya (ACMK)

Streamlines the practice of construction project management and sets professional codes of conduct for its members across Kenya. ACE's project management team operates under ACMK's code — meaning every project we manage is governed by nationally recognised standards of practice, reporting discipline and client obligation from mobilisation through to handover.

Professional Code of Conduct PM Standards Client Accountability

Architectural Association of Kenya (AAK)

An umbrella body connecting contractors with architects, quantity surveyors and structural engineers across Kenya. All ACE-employed architects hold current AAK registration — required by law to sign and stamp architectural drawings for regulatory submission. AAK registration ensures our design team operates under a nationally enforced code of professional ethics with ongoing CPD requirements.

Registered Architects CPD Compliant Drawing Certification

The premier professional society for civil, structural and mechanical engineers operating within the built environment in Kenya. All structural and civil engineers on the ACE team are IEK-affiliated — ensuring every design we produce, every calculation we certify and every structure we erect meets the highest professional engineering standard in the country.
