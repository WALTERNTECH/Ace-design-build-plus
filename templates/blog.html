OUR JOURNEY

6 Years. 50+ Projects. One Vision.

Key milestones that define ACE — Adaptive City Ecosystems — since founding in Nairobi in 2020.

2020 — FOUNDING

ACE — Adaptive City Ecosystems Established

Founded in Nairobi with a vision to eliminate the fragmentation between architecture, engineering and construction — bringing all three disciplines under one accountable team.

2021 — FIRST COMMERCIAL

First Major Commercial Project — Parklands Office Block

ACE completed its first Grade-A commercial office block in Parklands, delivering 12,000 sq ft of premium office space and establishing our reputation in the commercial sector.

2024 — 15 PROJECTS

25 Projects Milestone — Interior Division Launched

Reaching 25 completed projects, ACE formally launched its Interior Fit-Out division, enabling true end-to-end delivery from architectural concept through to furnished handover.

2025 — EXPANSION

Structural Engineering Division Launched In-House

ACE added in-house Structural Engineering capabilities, giving clients one truly integrated team from concept design through structural analysis and construction delivery.

2026 — NOW

50+ Projects. Emerald Heights & Sora Hub Under Delivery.

Today ACE manages its largest ever project portfolio — Emerald Heights, Sora Business Complex and five other active sites simultaneously, with 60+ professionals across all disciplines.
