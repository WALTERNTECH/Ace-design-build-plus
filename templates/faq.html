01 — General

About the firm

What is ACE Design + Build Partners?

ACE — Adaptive City Ecosystems Ltd — is an integrated design-build firm offering architectural design, structural and MEP engineering, construction management and premium interior fit-out across East Africa. All four disciplines are staffed in-house.

Where are you located?

Our office is on Tebere Crescent Road, Nairobi, Kenya. We deliver projects across Kenya and into neighbouring East African countries, and have masterplanned industrial facilities as far as the Horn of Africa.

What types of projects do you handle?

Residential developments, commercial buildings, mixed-use projects, office spaces, hospitality, schools, luxury interiors and industrial facilities. Our current load runs from a five-bedroom private villa to a 42,000 sq ft commercial complex.

Are you licensed and registered?

Yes. ACE holds NCA Category 4 registration and our professionals are registered or affiliated with AAK, IEK, ACMK, KABCEC and BORAQS. We provide registration numbers and certificates on request for any due-diligence process.

02 — Our Process

How a project runs

How does your project workflow operate?

Five stages: consultation and feasibility, design and approvals, costing and contract, construction, then handover and support. You always know which stage you are in and what has to close before the next one opens.

Do you assist with approvals?

Yes. We handle planning approvals, construction permits and regulatory documentation. Our architects are AAK-registered, which is what allows drawings to be signed and stamped for statutory submission.

Do you provide project timelines?

Every project is issued with a structured programme, a milestone plan and a reporting framework. If a milestone is going to slip, you hear it from us before it slips — not at handover.

Can I build from abroad?

Yes — our Diaspora Real Estate programme is built for exactly that. You get monthly photographic and written progress reports, drawdowns released against verified completed work, and one named contact throughout.

03 — Services

What we cover

Can ACE handle both design and construction?

Yes — that is the point of the firm. We operate as a complete design-build practice, so one team carries a project from first site visit to final key with no handover between consultants and contractor.

What software do you design in?

Modern BIM tooling, including Autodesk Revit and BIM 360, plus 3D visualisation and collaborative coordination platforms. Digital coordination lets us resolve clashes before they become variations on site.

Do you offer interior design?

Yes. Our interior division handles office fit-outs, luxury residential interiors, furniture planning, lighting design, custom joinery and premium finishes.

Can I engage you for only one stage?

Yes. Many clients start with design or with a single discipline and hand us the rest once they have seen how the programme holds. You are not obliged to take the full scope.

04 — Construction

On site

How do you ensure quality on site?

Experienced site engineers, scheduled inspections, documented reporting and strict construction standards — under a PMP®-certified construction manager working to ACMK's code of professional conduct.

Do you provide cost estimates?

Yes. Our BORAQS-registered quantity surveyors produce feasibility estimates, cost plans, bills of quantities and procurement reporting at every stage — so the contract price rests on measured work rather than a guess.

Can you manage large-scale projects?

Yes. NCA Category 4 registration authorises us to self-perform large-scale building works, and we currently run four major sites concurrently including an 18-storey tower and a 42,000 sq ft commercial complex.

How do you handle site safety?

A Zero Harm culture enforced daily through strict HSE standards. Our KABCEC membership also places us under negotiated Collective Bargaining Agreements, which protects both our workers and our clients' legal exposure.

05 — Interior Fit-Outs

Fit-out specifics

How long does an interior fit-out take?

Typically 8–12 weeks, depending on scope, material procurement lead times and site readiness. We will tell you which of those three is the binding constraint on your particular job.

Do you handle MEP modifications?

Yes. We coordinate HVAC, electrical, plumbing and data infrastructure modifications during fit-out — using our own IEK-affiliated MEP engineers rather than a subcontracted consultant.

Do you supply furniture and finishes?

Yes — furniture sourcing, custom joinery, lighting systems and premium finish selection, specified in writing so what arrives matches what was agreed.
