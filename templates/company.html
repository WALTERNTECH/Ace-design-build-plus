Our Journey

6 Years. One Vision.

The key milestones that have defined ACE — Adaptive City Ecosystems — since founding in Nairobi. A decade of building, innovating and delivering without compromise.

2020 — Founding

ACE — Adaptive City Ecosystems Established, Nairobi

Founded by four architects with a shared vision: eliminate the costly fragmentation between architecture, engineering and construction. ACE was built to deliver all three under one accountable team from day one.

2021 — First Commercial

First Grade-A Commercial Delivery — Parklands Office Block

ACE completed its first major commercial office block in Parklands — 12,000 sq ft of premium office space delivered on time and on budget. This project established our commercial reputation across Nairobi and attracted a new tier of developer clients.

2022 — Interior Division

25 Projects Milestone + Interior Fit-Out Division Launched

Reaching 25 completed projects, ACE formally launched its Interior Fit-Out Division — enabling true end-to-end delivery from architectural concept through to fully furnished, handed-over spaces. The division now handles corporate, hospitality and residential fit-outs.

2023 — First Major Award

Best Residential Project — Architecture Awards Kenya

ACE's first major industry award for Karen Ridge Villas — recognised for design excellence, sustainability integration and exceptional quality of finish. The award validated ACE's premium positioning and opened doors to larger institutional clients.

2024 — Diaspora Programme

Diaspora Real Estate Portfolio Programme Launched

ACE launched its dedicated Diaspora Real Estate Programme, targeting Kenyan investors seeking trusted, end-to-end managed construction investment back home. Monthly reporting, transparent financial management and remote client access — designed specifically for the diaspora investor's needs.

2025 — Industrial Masterplanning

Gold Plant Masterplan — Awdal Region, Somaliland

ACE undertook the masterplanning of a high-capacity 500 TPD gold processing facility in the Awdal region, Somaliland — coordinating systems integration of a 25 TPH Ball Mill and 2,000-ton CIL circuit, targeting commissioning-to-production within 45 days and a projected $100M+ annual revenue.

2026 — Today

50+ Projects. 4 Active Major Sites. East Africa's Integrated Design-Build Leader.

Today ACE simultaneously delivers Emerald Heights, Sora Business Complex, XYZ Apartments and the Westlands Commercial Project — the firm's largest ever concurrent project load. 60+ professionals across all disciplines. 15+ industry awards. Registered with every major professional body. Zero compromise.

National Construction Authority (NCA)
The NCA is the mandatory state regulatory body in Kenya. All local contractors must be licensed by the NCA to legally operate, bid on, or execute building and civil engineering projects. ACE holds an active NCA Category 4 registration — the classification that authorises us to self-perform large-scale building works across Kenya. Our NCA licence is renewed annually and is independently verifiable through the Authority's public contractor register.
