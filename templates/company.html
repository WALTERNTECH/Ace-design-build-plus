Our Journey

Six years,
one direction.

The milestones that shaped ACE since founding in Nairobi — each one a capability we added because a project demanded it.

2020Founding

ACE — Adaptive City Ecosystems established, Nairobi

Founded by four architects with a shared conviction: eliminate the costly fragmentation between architecture, engineering and construction. ACE was built to deliver all three under one accountable team from day one.

2021First Commercial

First Grade-A commercial delivery — Parklands Office Block

12,000 sq ft of premium office space delivered on time and on budget. The project established our commercial reputation across Nairobi and brought in a new tier of developer clients.

2022Interior Division

25 projects, and the Interior Fit-Out Division launched

Reaching 25 completed projects, ACE formally launched its Interior Fit-Out Division — enabling true end-to-end delivery from architectural concept through to a fully furnished, handed-over space.

2023Recognition

Best Residential Project — Architecture Awards Kenya

Our first major industry award, for Karen Ridge Villas — recognised for design excellence, sustainability integration and quality of finish.

2024Diaspora Programme

Diaspora Real Estate Programme launched

A dedicated programme for Kenyan investors building from abroad: monthly reporting, transparent financial management and remote client access, designed around clients who cannot walk the site.

2025Industrial Masterplanning

Gold plant masterplan — Awdal region, Somaliland

Masterplanning of a 500 TPD gold processing facility, coordinating systems integration of a 25 TPH ball mill and a 2,000-ton CIL circuit, targeting commissioning to production within 45 days.

2026Today

50+ projects, four major sites running concurrently

ACE is currently delivering Emerald Heights, Sora Business Complex, XYZ Apartments and the Westlands Commercial Project — the firm's largest concurrent project load to date, across every discipline in-house.

Regulated · Certified · Accountable

Credentials you can
check yourself.

Every registration is current and renewed on schedule. We provide registration numbers and certificates on request for any due-diligence process.

NCACategory 4
National Construction Authority

The mandatory state regulator. Category 4 authorises ACE to self-perform large-scale building works across Kenya. Verifiable on the Authority's public contractor register.

AAKRegistered
Architectural Association of Kenya

Every ACE architect holds current AAK registration — required by law to stamp architectural drawings for regulatory submission.

IEKAffiliated
Institution of Engineers of Kenya

Structural, civil and MEP engineers on the ACE team are IEK-affiliated, meeting the national professional engineering standard.

ACMKMember
Association of Construction Managers of Kenya

Project management operates under ACMK's professional code — nationally recognised standards for reporting discipline and client obligation.

KABCECMember
Kenya Association of Building & Civil Engineering Contractors

Membership places ACE under negotiated Collective Bargaining Agreements and standard Conditions of Contract for building works.

BORAQSRegistered
Board of Registration of Architects & Quantity Surveyors

Our quantity surveyors are BORAQS-registered, so bills of quantities and valuations carry professional standing.
